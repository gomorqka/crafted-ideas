What we do

Designed, built and fitted by the same hands.

Bespoke kitchens are most of what we do — specified, built and installed end to end. A kitchen never stands alone, so the trades around it are in-house too. The same standard applies whether it is a full installation or a single door that will not sit right.

Kitchens — the core

Kitchen design, construction, installation & fitting

  • ConstructionFull builds and structural alterations — walls, floors, services, ready for the fit.
  • InstallationComplete kitchen installs — units, worktops, appliances, plumbing and finishing.
  • FittingPrecision fitting of cabinetry and worktops. Flush, level, and made to last.
  • RepairsDoors, hinges, runners, worktops, water damage — fixed properly, not patched.
