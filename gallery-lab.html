Gallery lab
Same nine photographs in all three. None of them stack or pin, so none should flicker.

A · Swipe

One photograph per screen, side by side. Swipe on a phone or tablet, arrows and arrow keys on a desktop. The browser only ever holds two frames next to each other, so there is nothing to evict and nothing to pin.

B · Grid, then expand

All nine at a glance as thumbnails, any one of them opening full-screen. Only the small files load up front — about a tenth of the bytes — and the big one is fetched on demand.

C · One per screen, sides alternate

Full-bleed, one photograph per screen, in ordinary flow — no sticky stacking. The caption changes side each time so the eye keeps moving. Closest to what is live now, without the mechanism that caused the trouble.
